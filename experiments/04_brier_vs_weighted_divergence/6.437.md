The files there are the course lecture PDFs (about 19 MB, 28 lectures). `ultimate-utils` is a public GitHub repository and a PyPI package, so the PDFs are not committed here.

Lecture 13, `13. InferenceAsDecision.pdf`, is the one that matches the notes on log loss and the quadratic cost.

- The quadratic cost sums `(q(a) - 1{a = x})^2` over the whole alphabet. That is the Brier score.
- Log loss `-log q(x)` is local: the cost of a forecast depends on the forecast only through the probability of the symbol that occurred.
- A cost is proper when the expected cost is minimized by reporting the true distribution.
- For an alphabet of at least three symbols, log loss is the only smooth cost that is both local and proper (Theorem 13.1 in that lecture).

The numerical check of what that implies for the weighted squared divergence is in `README.md` next to this file.
